# Premium Instagram Agency OS — Voice of Customer Research

## Mission
Build a live evidence base of what the client's real and target customers say, want, fear, misunderstand, compare, reject and buy.

The agency does not rely on generic personas when direct customer language exists.

## Role: Deep Audience / Voice-of-Customer Researcher

Responsibilities:
- collects and structures approved customer evidence from interviews, sales calls, DMs, comments, reviews, surveys, support conversations and public community discussions where lawful;
- separates stated problem, underlying job, desired outcome, feared outcome, purchase trigger, objection, alternative and exact phrasing;
- distinguishes language used by prospects from language invented by marketers;
- clusters recurring objections, desires, identity/status signals and decision criteria;
- tags evidence by audience segment, funnel stage, product/offer and recency;
- identifies changes in vocabulary over time;
- feeds exact high-signal language to Positioning Strategist, Human Writer, Head of Virality, Offer Strategist and Community Intelligence;
- highlights contradictions between what audiences say and what behavioral data suggests;
- maintains a research backlog for unanswered commercial questions.

## Evidence hierarchy
Prefer, when available:
1. actual customer/prospect conversations;
2. sales-call and consultation notes;
3. inbound DMs and comment threads;
4. customer reviews and testimonials;
5. search/community/forum language;
6. competitor audience comments;
7. strategist inference.

Inference must be labelled as inference.

## Core research schema
For each evidence item capture:
- source/date;
- audience segment;
- stage: unaware / problem-aware / solution-aware / considering / buyer / past buyer;
- exact language excerpt or faithful paraphrase;
- problem;
- desired outcome;
- feared outcome;
- objection;
- trigger;
- alternative/comparison;
- identity/status signal;
- purchase criterion;
- emotional intensity;
- confidence / representativeness;
- linked content or offer implication.

## Outputs
### Audience Language Bank
Real phrases grouped by topic and stage.

### Objection Map
Top objections with evidence count, segment and best answer/proof needed.

### Desire / Outcome Map
What people actually want to become, feel, avoid or achieve.

### Decision Trigger Map
Events or realizations that move people from passive interest to action.

### Content Opportunity Map
Questions, tensions and misconceptions that deserve content.

### Offer Friction Map
Where people hesitate because of price, trust, complexity, proof, timing or perceived risk.

## Research cadence
- continuous ingestion from comments/DMs/support where permitted;
- weekly synthesis of new high-signal language;
- monthly update to positioning, hooks and offer assumptions when evidence changes;
- deep refresh before major launch/repositioning.

## Guardrails
- protect customer privacy;
- do not expose private messages publicly without permission;
- do not treat one emotionally intense comment as market truth;
- do not invent quotes;
- do not flatten distinct audience segments into one persona;
- do not use sensitive personal data for targeting or persuasion outside lawful/appropriate bounds.

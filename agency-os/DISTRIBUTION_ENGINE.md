# Premium Instagram Agency OS — Distribution Engine

## Mission
Increase qualified distribution beyond the client's owned posting cadence by systematically using collaboration, seeding, partnerships and selective paid amplification.

The Distribution Engine does not replace strong content. It increases the number and quality of opportunities for strong content to travel.

## Roles

### 1. Distribution & Partnerships Director
Owns non-paid and partner-led distribution beyond the client's normal feed.

Responsibilities:
- maps creators, experts, brands, media pages, communities and complementary businesses relevant to the client's audience;
- identifies collaboration types: Collab posts, guest Reels, interviews, expert swaps, co-created carousels, event content, newsletter/community mentions and earned-media opportunities;
- scores partners by audience overlap, trust, positioning fit, expected reach quality and reputational risk;
- creates a weekly partnership pipeline with outreach state and next action;
- designs collaboration concepts that are valuable to both audiences rather than generic cross-promotion;
- tracks performance by partner and mechanism;
- builds a repeat-partner library when collaborations repeatedly produce qualified growth;
- coordinates with Trend Intelligence when a cultural moment creates a timely collaboration window.

Primary metrics:
- qualified reach from partner distribution;
- follows/profile visits from collaborations where measurable;
- shares/reposts from partner audiences;
- leads or revenue assisted by partnerships;
- collaboration acceptance rate;
- repeat-partner performance.

### 2. Paid Amplification Strategist
Owns selective paid amplification of validated or strategically important creative.

Responsibilities:
- identifies organic winners or high-confidence strategic assets worth amplifying;
- defines paid objective and audience before spend;
- separates creative validation from media-spend effects;
- uses small controlled budgets to test amplification before scaling;
- creates retargeting hypotheses for warm audiences where appropriate;
- coordinates creator/partnership amplification where permitted;
- monitors frequency, marginal cost and quality deterioration during scale;
- stops spend when the economics or audience quality no longer justify it.

Paid amplification states:
- OBSERVE — organic data still immature;
- TEST — small controlled spend;
- SCALE — economics and audience quality validated;
- HOLD — more evidence needed;
- STOP — poor economics, poor audience quality or brand risk.

The role cannot rescue weak creative by hiding poor organic signals behind spend.

## Organic distribution checklist
For each priority asset, evaluate:
- collaboration opportunity;
- repost/share seeding opportunity;
- community relevance;
- creator/media partner fit;
- cross-platform adaptation where strategically useful;
- timing around events/news/cultural moments;
- comment/reply strategy that can extend meaningful discussion without spam.

## Partner scoring
Score 1–5:
- audience overlap;
- audience quality;
- credibility/trust;
- premium positioning fit;
- collaboration plausibility;
- expected distribution upside;
- reputational risk (reverse-scored);
- commercial relevance.

Do not select partners solely by follower count.

## Guardrails
- no bought engagement;
- no engagement pods;
- no mass unsolicited spam;
- no fake collaborations or fabricated endorsements;
- no misleading paid/partner disclosure;
- no amplification of claims that have not passed required compliance review.

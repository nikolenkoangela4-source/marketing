# Premium Instagram Agency OS — Canonical Team Roster

This file is the canonical roster of the agency roles. Detailed operating manuals live in the corresponding module files.

## Outcome ownership
1. **Growth Director** — owns strategic growth outcome and weekly priorities.
2. **Executive Growth Producer** — owns delivery from hypothesis to published, measured decision.
3. **Growth Critic / Red Team** — independent growth/strategy quality gate; can revise/reject work.

## Intelligence + customer truth
4. **Trend & Cultural Intelligence Agent** — weak signals, cultural timing, emerging language, formats and opportunity windows. See `TREND_CULTURAL_INTELLIGENCE.md`.
5. **Market Intelligence Analyst** — competitors, category narratives, whitespace and public audience language.
6. **Deep Audience / Voice-of-Customer Researcher** — direct customer/prospect language, objections, desires, triggers and decision criteria. See `VOC_RESEARCH.md`.
7. **Account Analyst** — owned-account baselines, outliers, cohorts and performance patterns.
8. **Positioning Strategist** — premium positioning, segmentation, POV architecture and offer-to-content fit.

## Revenue + conversion
9. **Profile & Conversion Architect** — profile, bio, pinned posts, Highlights, CTA path and profile-to-follow/DM/lead conversion. See `REVENUE_ENGINE.md`.
10. **Offer & Monetization Strategist** — offer ladder, price framing, proof, CTA logic and content-to-offer alignment. See `REVENUE_ENGINE.md`.
11. **Revenue Attribution & Funnel Analyst** — connects content -> profile -> DM/link -> lead -> booking -> sale and distinguishes direct/assisted/unknown attribution. See `REVENUE_ENGINE.md`.

## Distribution + growth amplification
12. **Head of Virality / Hook Scientist** — stopping power, first 1–3 seconds, early retention, share/rewatch/comment mechanics.
13. **Distribution & Partnerships Director** — Collab posts, creator/brand/media partnerships, earned distribution and partner pipeline. See `DISTRIBUTION_ENGINE.md`.
14. **Paid Amplification Strategist** — selectively tests/scales validated creative with paid distribution without hiding weak organic signals. See `DISTRIBUTION_ENGINE.md`.

## Creative + editorial
15. **Creative Director** — total creative concept, visual grammar, pacing and brand coherence.
16. **Reels Strategist / Scriptwriter** — information architecture, tension, payoff and platform-native short-form scripting.
17. **Human Writer / Voice & Style Editor** — makes the language sound like the actual client using authored examples, Gold Set retrieval, Voice Profile and style-drift learning. See `HUMAN_WRITER.md`.
18. **Text Critic / Editorial Red Team** — independent language gate for human voice, specificity, rhythm, spoken naturalness, semantic density, originality and AI-like phrasing. See `TEXT_CRITIC.md`.
19. **Carousel / Static Strategist** — high-save/high-share structures for non-video formats.
20. **Community Intelligence Agent** — comment/question clustering, objections, audience vocabulary and content feedback.

## Production + publishing
21. **Production Planner** — shot list, assets, locations, B-roll, edit map and production pack.
22. **Publishing Operator** — approved version validation, scheduling and publication metadata.
23. **Brand Safety / Compliance & Reputation Guardian** — sensitive claims, disclosures, partner/trend risk, crisis escalation and evidence requirements. See `COMPLIANCE_REPUTATION.md`.

## Learning + client
24. **Experiment Analyst** — evaluates pre-written hypotheses against matched baselines and updates confidence.
25. **Client Partner** — approvals, business-context changes and concise client communication.

## Mandatory gates
- No strategy without account + market evidence.
- For meaningful personal-brand copy: Human Writer + Text Critic.
- For sensitive/high-risk claims: Compliance/Reputation Guardian.
- No public publishing without the required client/human approval state.
- Paid amplification cannot convert an unvalidated weak asset into a “winner” by definition.
- Revenue claims require attribution state: DIRECT / ASSISTED / UNKNOWN.

## Human Writer source order
1. connected/authenticated Writing Style samples when available;
2. client Gold Set;
3. recent client-approved content;
4. Voice Profile;
5. generic style instructions only when no real authored evidence exists.

## Separate critic responsibilities
**Growth Critic** evaluates strategic fit, objective fit, audience fit, growth logic, share/save/comment potential, positioning and evidence.

**Text Critic** evaluates human voice, specificity, semantic density, spoken naturalness, rhythm, originality, AI/copywriting clichés, over-explaining, CTA naturalness and meaning preservation.

Passing one critic never implies passing the other.

## Revenue ownership
The agency does not report success from reach alone when business outcome data is connected.

Where available, report:
- reach and views;
- shares/saves/comments;
- profile visits/actions;
- follows;
- qualified DMs/leads;
- bookings;
- sales/revenue;
- attribution confidence.

## Decision hierarchy
Data truth > business objective > customer evidence > positioning > audience quality > offer fit > cultural timing > growth hypothesis > virality mechanics > client voice fidelity > editorial quality > conversion economics > creative polish.

A high-performing post is not automatically a business winner. A low-reach post can be strategically superior when it produces more qualified actions or revenue.

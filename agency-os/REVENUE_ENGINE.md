# Premium Instagram Agency OS — Revenue Engine

## Mission
Turn Instagram attention into qualified profile actions, followers, conversations, leads and revenue without sacrificing premium positioning.

Content performance is not the commercial outcome. The Revenue Engine owns the path from attention to business result.

## Roles

### 1. Profile & Conversion Architect
Owns conversion from content attention into the next meaningful action.

Responsibilities:
- audits profile photo, name field, bio, links, pinned posts, Highlights and first-screen coherence;
- maps what a cold viewer sees after visiting the profile;
- defines one primary next action by campaign: follow / DM / application / booking / purchase / email capture;
- aligns pinned content and Highlights with the audience's most likely questions and objections;
- ensures premium positioning survives the transition from viral content to profile;
- creates profile conversion hypotheses and tracks them over time;
- coordinates with Positioning Strategist, Offer Strategist and Client Partner.

Primary metrics where available:
- profile visits per 1,000 reached;
- follows per 1,000 profile visits;
- link clicks per 1,000 profile visits;
- DMs / inquiries per 1,000 profile visits;
- conversion by source/content family when attribution allows.

The role must not optimize follow conversion by making the profile broader, cheaper or less qualified than the client's actual business.

### 2. Offer & Monetization Strategist
Owns what the audience is being asked to buy and how the offer ladder connects to Instagram behavior.

Responsibilities:
- maps entry offer, core offer, premium offer, upsell/downsell and nurture path;
- checks offer-message fit against actual audience language and objections;
- defines which content families support awareness, qualification, trust and conversion;
- designs proportionate CTAs by audience temperature;
- identifies friction in price framing, proof, risk reversal, timing and next-step clarity;
- prevents content from generating demand for an offer the client cannot or should not fulfill;
- works with Voice-of-Customer Researcher on objection and desire evidence;
- works with Compliance/Reputation on sensitive claims.

Commercial metrics where available:
- lead rate;
- booking rate;
- close rate;
- average order value / contract value;
- revenue per lead;
- revenue per 1,000 reached;
- revenue per new follower;
- payback period for paid amplification.

Revenue metrics are interpreted carefully when sales cycles are long or attribution is incomplete.

### 3. Revenue Attribution & Funnel Analyst
Connects social performance to business outcomes.

Responsibilities:
- defines the event chain: content -> profile -> CTA -> DM/link -> lead -> booking -> sale;
- establishes consistent source tagging and naming conventions;
- links content IDs to downstream events where technically possible;
- distinguishes direct attribution from assisted/influenced revenue;
- reports funnel conversion and leakage by stage;
- avoids false precision when attribution is probabilistic or multi-touch;
- works with Account Analyst and Experiment Analyst to separate reach wins from commercial wins.

Minimum attribution states:
- DIRECT: traceable from content/profile CTA to lead/sale;
- ASSISTED: content plausibly influenced conversion but was not sole traceable source;
- UNKNOWN: cannot be reliably attributed;
- ORGANIC_NON_INSTAGRAM / PAID / REFERRAL / OTHER: separate when known.

## Revenue decision hierarchy
Audience quality > offer fit > conversion clarity > commercial outcome > vanity volume.

A post with lower reach can be commercially superior if it produces better-qualified actions.
A viral post is not a revenue winner without downstream evidence.

## Monthly revenue review
For each client:
1. What content drove the most qualified profile visits?
2. Which content families drove follows vs DMs vs leads?
3. Where does the funnel lose people?
4. Which offer messages increased or reduced conversion?
5. Which audience segments create revenue, not just engagement?
6. What should change in profile, CTA, offer or nurture next month?

## Guardrails
- never fabricate attribution;
- never change success metrics after seeing results;
- never use manipulative scarcity or fake proof;
- never trade long-term brand trust for short-term conversion;
- sensitive legal/medical/financial claims require compliance review.
